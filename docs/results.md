# Results, proofs and calculations

The [supplement](../supplement/supplement.pdf) gives the mathematical arguments and the interpretation of each finite witness. The table below connects those arguments with the files and commands needed to check them. Paths are relative to the repository directory.

| Mathematical assertion | Proof or explanation | Data | Command and expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The seed $F_1$ has strict level one; deleting the centre of $F_2$ leaves a dismantlable graph. | Article §§3 and 5; supplement Appendix A. | `data/family_3k8.json`, `data/article_tables/`, `data/article_remainder.json` | `python reproduce.py --only article`: 11 vertices, 33 seed edges, 10 outer deletions, 23 inner folds, and 12 remainder folds; all pass. |
| The thirteen non-central neighbourhoods of $F_2$ have non-zero $q$. | Article §5; supplement Appendix A. | `data/article_tables/seed_cliques.tex` | The same command: 13 complete clique rows; no cliques above cardinality four. |
| The figures represent the stated incidences. | Article Figures 1–3; supplement Appendix A. | `figures/first_layer.tikz`, `figures/seed_complement.tikz`, `figures/layers.tikz` | The same command: 8 schematic incidences expand to 32 added edges; 22 complement edges; 30 edges in the three-layer diagram. |
| $H_1,H_2,H_3$ have strict levels $1,2,3$, and the first uniform remainder belongs to $\mathcal D_3$. | Article §7; supplement §4. | `data/certificates/` | `python reproduce.py --only 4k4`: 7 accepted roots, the induced-prefix checks and the direct proof for $H_1$. |
| All nineteen non-central neighbourhoods in the first uniform extension of $H_k$ have non-zero $q$. | Supplement §4, clique table. | `FINITE_CERTIFICATE.json.gz` in `data/certificates/`; `supplement/tables/4k4/cliques.tex` | The same command: 19 rows agree with independent clique enumeration and deletion recurrence, in every degree. |
| The twelve-vertex seed for $J_k$ has strict level one; its remainder is in $\mathcal D_1$. | Supplement §5. | `data/seed_6k6_original.json`, `supplement/tables/6k6/` | `python reproduce.py --only 6k6`: 12 outer deletions, 14 inner folds, 12 negative degree rows, 17 clique rows. |
| Removing $\{1,2\}$ from $F_1$ gives the stated kernel; its neighbourhood homology determines the first-layer obstructions. | Supplement §6. | `data/family_3k8.json` | `python reproduce.py --only kernels`: 32 kernel edges; zero reduced homology of the kernel; neighbourhood $\beta_1=1$ except $\beta_1=2$ at vertex 10; the stated degree shifts. |

| The two constructions of each family agree at the tested levels. | Supplement §3 and the definitions of $F_k,H_k,J_k$. | `data/family_*.json` | `python reproduce.py --only examples`: exact adjacency agreement for 18 graphs, levels 1–6 in each family. |
| A single centre deletion from $F_k$ is dismantlable, while any other single deletion has non-zero $q$. | Article §6. | The constructed graphs $F_2,F_3,F_4$. | `python reproduce.py --only deletions`: all single-vertex deletions at these three levels pass their stated test. The article proves the result for every $k\geq2$. |
| Invalid certificate data are rejected. | Supplement §4; [certificate rules](certificates.md). | Deliberately altered copies, created in memory. | `python reproduce.py --only mutations`: 12 rejected alterations, including graph incidences, roots, levels, dependencies and coefficients. |
| The general layer and kernel identities hold on the additional finite ranges. | Supplement §§3, 6 and 7. | All labelled inputs within the ranges in the [guide](reproduction.md). | `python reproduce.py --extended`: 1,170 layer templates, 23,850 Euler identities, 1,386 homological shifts and 237 covered profile pairs. |

The order formulas and the strict levels for all $k$ follow from the construction theorem and the verified finite hypotheses. The extended calculations check the identities on the specified finite sets; they do not replace the proofs. Likewise, acyclicity and a zero value of $q$ are never used as sufficient tests for membership in a dismantling class.
