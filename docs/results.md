# Results, proofs and calculations

The [supplement](../supplement/supplement.pdf) gives the mathematical arguments and the interpretation of each finite witness. The table below connects those arguments with the files and commands needed to check them. References to article sections, figures and results concern the identified 23-page manuscript described in [ARTICLE.md](../ARTICLE.md). Paths are relative to the repository directory. We write $\widetilde\beta_t$ for the dimension of reduced homology in degree $t$, over $\mathbb F_2$.

The order formulas and the strict levels for all $k$ follow from the construction theorem and the verified finite hypotheses. The extended calculations check the identities on the specified finite sets; they do not replace the proofs. No program infers membership from acyclicity or a zero value of $q$. The proof of the minimum uses the separately established small-order classification only for non-empty graphs of order at most eight.
