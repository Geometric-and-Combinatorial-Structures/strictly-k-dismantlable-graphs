# The accompanying article

**Linear-size graphs at every strict dismantling level**

Jesús F. Espinoza, Martín Eduardo Frías-Armenta, Rosalía G. Hernández-Amador and Héctor A. Hernández-Hernández.

**Article reference:** [arXiv:2611.14430](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14430).

This repository contains the supplementary proofs, graph data and finite calculations associated with the work. The notation agrees with the article: $F_k$ has $3k+8$ vertices and $H_k$ has $4k+4$ vertices, for every $k\geq1$.

Release **v1.1.0** also contains the finite verification of $m_2=12$. Its mathematical reduction and all required data appear in §8 of the [supplement](supplement/supplement.pdf). The article version currently available at the locator above, **v1 of 10 November 2026**, does not contain this additional result. The verification tables agree with the authors' 23-page manuscript of 12 November 2026, identified by SHA-256 `c2b1061ed81cb52f9ede74dd1ddbf998d9840cff0e8ae6921cf44fb8634c596d`. [The source correspondence](docs/article-correspondence.md) records the precise relationship.

References to article sections, figures and results in this repository use that identified 23-page manuscript. The correspondence record also identifies a copy whose only changes update the repository citation.
