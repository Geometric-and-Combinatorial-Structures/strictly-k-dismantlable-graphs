# The accompanying article

**Linear-size graphs at every strict dismantling level**

Jesús F. Espinoza, Martín Eduardo Frías-Armenta, Rosalía G. Hernández-Amador and Héctor A. Hernández-Hernández.

This repository contains the supplementary mathematical arguments, explicit graph data and reproducible finite calculations associated with this work. The constructions and notation agree with the article: $F_k$ has $3k+8$ vertices and $H_k$ has $4k+4$ vertices, for every $k\geq1$.

**Article reference:** a public locator has not yet been assigned.

The [mathematical supplement](supplement/supplement.pdf) contains the definitions and proofs needed to read and reproduce the material in this repository.
